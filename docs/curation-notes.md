# Curation notes — Free Read-Aloud Directory

Maintained by Asdale Dynamics, LLC. These notes explain how entries were chosen
and what to re-check on the next review pass. Plain English on purpose.

## What this is

A freebie directory for people who would rather **listen** than read. It covers
four kinds of help:

1. **Browser read modes** — read-aloud features already built into a browser or office suite.
2. **Phone equivalents** — apps and accessibility features that turn a phone into a reader.
3. **Free TTS apps** — desktop, local, and web-based text-to-speech tools.
4. **E-readers with synthetic narration** — e-ink devices that speak a book.
5. A small **bonus** shelf of free *human*-narration listening (library apps), clearly flagged because they are not TTS.

Every entry carries four fields:

- **Platform** — where it runs.
- **Offline** — Yes / Partial / No, with the catch spelled out.
- **What it checks** — the practical things a reader should confirm on their own setup, since features change and vary by model, plan, and region.
- **Verdict** — one plain-English sentence, warm and honest.

## Selection criteria

An entry earns a place if it is:

- **Free** — no paid plan required to do the core job (free *tiers* are allowed but the limits are stated).
- **Real** — it works today for an ordinary reader without a credit card, developer account, or subscription.
- **Honest** — we list the friction (upgrades, online requirement, model restrictions) rather than hiding it.

## Deliberately excluded

- **ElevenLabs / premium AI voices** — free tiers are generous but attribution and commercial-use terms make them awkward "freebies" for casual listening.
- **Speechify** — heavy upsell pressure; the free tier is more marketing than tool.
- **Voice Dream Reader** — excellent, but paid software.
- **Anything that needs hardware purchase** (dedicated reader devices like the Victor Reader) — out of scope for a "free" directory.
- **Paid audiobook services** (Audible plans) covered in the *ai-narration-choices-guide* instead.

## How we disambiguate from sibling projects

- **ai-narration-choices-guide** — for *authors* deciding how their audiobook gets made (AI narrator vs. human narrator vs. contract terms). This directory is for *readers* who want to listen to text they already have, free.
- **free-large-print-tech-kit** — for *print/visual* accessibility: large print, e-ink sizing, screen magnification, and reading glasses. This directory is for *audio* instead of print.

If a tool could go either way, it lives in both with a cross-reference note.

## Review cadence

Feature names move around (Chrome renamed its reader feature, Kindle changed
accessibility options between models). Every entry's **"what it checks"** field
is written so a future reviewer can re-verify in under five minutes per tool.
Recommended re-check: every 6 months, or after a major browser/OS release.
